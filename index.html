ARTHA AI

An AI research platform for Indian stocks on NSE and BSE. It runs an agent workflow, scores each stock, writes a 13-section dossier, and optimizes portfolios with real mean-variance math.

The problem

Retail investors in India piece together prices, ratios, news and macro data from many sites, and generic chatbots can invent numbers. Free market APIs also fail often, and buy or sell calls are regulated by SEBI.

What it does

Agent workflow
A LangGraph workflow plans the task, fetches market, fundamental, news and macro data, runs analytics, scores the stock, then applies validation and compliance steps.
Research dossier
Thirteen sections plus a disclaimer, with a streamed agent timeline and PDF export.
Scoring
Technical 25%, Fundamental 25%, Risk 20%, Sentiment 15% and Macro 15%, combined into a score from 0 to 100.
Risk and technicals
RSI, MACD, moving averages, Bollinger Bands, beta against NIFTY, VaR, CVaR and max drawdown.
Portfolio and MPT
Long-only mean-variance optimizer with Ledoit-Wolf covariance, a computed efficient frontier, Min Risk, Max Sharpe and Balanced modes, and beta-based what-if shocks.
Calculators
Black-Scholes-Merton options with Greeks, futures fair value, bond price, duration and yield.
Alerts and monitoring
Rule-based alerts, provider health, circuit breaker state and Prometheus-style metrics.

The portfolio optimizer

  • Expected returns come from live price history and are shrunk halfway toward the cross-stock average, because short windows are noisy.
  • The covariance matrix uses Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage, and a 30% per-stock cap keeps the portfolio diversified.
  • Max Sharpe is found by sweeping risk aversion along the frontier.
  • Tests check a closed-form two-asset case and confirm the result beats 3,000 random portfolios.
  • The portfolio tab is live-data only. If prices or history are missing, it says so instead of showing sample numbers.

Engineering notes

  • Testing against known answers showed that an early placeholder optimizer was not real mean-variance, so it was replaced.
  • I found and fixed a sign bug that pulled sample prices down about 70%, and a precedence bug that made sample highs and lows about 1/100 of price.
  • Report wording avoids buy or sell calls and target prices, and a disclaimer appears on every view and PDF.
